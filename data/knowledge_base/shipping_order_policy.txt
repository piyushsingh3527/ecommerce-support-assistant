# SHIPPING AND ORDER POLICY

## 1. Order Processing

All successfully placed orders are processed by the system. Order processing begins after the order is confirmed.

Customers can view their order details through the order management system.

## 2. Order Tracking

Customers can check their order status using their Order ID.

The available order statuses are:

- Pending: The order has been placed and is waiting for confirmation.
- Confirmed: The order has been accepted.
- Shipped: The order has been dispatched.
- Delivered: The order has been successfully delivered.

Customers can contact the support team if they need assistance with their order status.

## 3. Order Cancellation

Customers should contact the support team as soon as possible if they want to cancel an order.

Cancellation requests are subject to the order's current status and the applicable cancellation rules.

## 4. Delivery Information

Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location and shipping conditions.

Customers should provide a correct shipping address and contact information while placing an order.

## 5. Payment Information

The current application uses a simulated checkout process.

It does not process real payments or connect to an actual payment gateway.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Products

Customers who receive a damaged or incorrect product should contact customer support and provide their Order ID along with a description of the issue.

The support team can review the complaint and assist the customer according to the applicable return policy.

## 7. Product Availability

Product availability depends on the stock information maintained in the system.

An order can only be processed when sufficient stock is available.
